Your data

Records are kept in this browser on this device. No accounts or bank connections. If you clear Safari website data, delete the app, or change devices, records may be lost unless you back them up. Before installing an update, export a fresh backup to iCloud Drive.

Backups include all transactions, recurring schedules, and skipped due dates in readable form. Keep them somewhere private. Import replaces the current data after confirmation. Older v1 and v2 backups are still supported but contain no recurring schedules.
